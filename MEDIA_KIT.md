# Media kit / 미디어 키트

## Research at a glance

**Title:** Variant-level concordance can overstate exact peptide–HLA shortlist reproducibility: a 63-condition HCC1395 audit

This work is a methods and reproducibility audit of publicly available HCC1395/HCC1395BL SEQC2 data. It asks whether agreement measured at the variant level can overstate agreement for the exact variant–peptide–HLA units that downstream prioritization pipelines actually use.

The manuscript has been submitted to *Computational Biology and Chemistry* and is currently with the editor. It has **not** yet been peer reviewed, accepted, or published.

## Verified findings

- 63 analytical conditions: 7 sites, 21 libraries nested within sites, and 3 aligners per library.
- At Top-34, median Jaccard similarity was 0.700 at variant level but 0.283 for exact peptide–HLA units.
- Median resolution-aware concordance loss was 0.399.
- Predictor agreement was 49%, with 58 of 114 decisions discordant at the 500-nM threshold.
- No exact public mass-spectrometry support was identified for the shortlisted units in the coverage-limited external audit.
- Final status: **NO_GO_FOR_CANDIDATE_NOMINATION**.

## Claim boundary

This work does **not** establish peptide presentation, immunogenicity, vaccine-target suitability, treatment efficacy, safety, or clinical utility. It does not nominate a biological or clinical candidate and should not be described as a cancer-vaccine discovery or validation study.

## 한국어 요약

이 연구는 공개 HCC1395/HCC1395BL SEQC2 자료를 이용해 네오안티젠 우선순위화 파이프라인의 분석 재현성을 점검한 방법론 연구다. 변이 수준 일치도 중앙값은 0.700이었지만 실제 후속 우선순위화 단위인 정확한 peptide–HLA 수준에서는 0.283으로 낮아졌고, 해상도 차이에 따른 일치도 손실 중앙값은 0.399였다. 이 결과는 암백신 후보의 발견·검증이나 임상적 효능을 의미하지 않으며, 최종 상태는 **NO_GO_FOR_CANDIDATE_NOMINATION**이다.
