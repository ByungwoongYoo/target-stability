# British Journal of Cancer tailoring — ambitious first submission option

## Title

Retain the neutral, non-conclusion title: **Cross-resource reproducibility and incremental validity of colorectal cancer dependency maps**. It is below the journal's 150-character limit and does not claim generic superiority. Suggested article category: an original Article positioned around cancer functional genomics and translational interpretation, not a clinical trial or new mechanism paper.

## Abstract emphasis

Lead with the oncological decision problem: what evidence is added when a strong 2D dependency is replicated in independent organoid resources? Preserve the positive corrected Phase III+ association alongside Phase II+ non-significance, exclusive-3D non-enrichment and uncertain pharmacologic advantage. Do not turn clinical development history into clinical validation.

The journal-neutral abstract is 227 words. BJC requires a structured abstract of at most 200 words; the following is a shorter tailoring option, not a replacement of the neutral master.

**Background:** Cross-resource reproducibility need not establish added validity beyond existing cancer models.

**Methods:** We compared aggregate colorectal dependency profiles from 18 Broad 3D screens, 67 Broad 2D screens and 81 non-overlapping Sanger models across 15,008 genes, alongside historical clinical precedence and independent organoid pharmacology.

**Results:** The 3D strongest-decile intersection contained 763 genes; 645 (84.53%) also ranked highly in 2D. Sanger concordance was 0.330 with Broad 3D and 0.353 with Broad 2D (difference −0.0232; conditional gene-bootstrap 95% interval −0.0330 to −0.0131). Among 994 selected strong-2D genes, all-three-profile membership was associated with historical Phase III+ precedence after the locked adjustment (OR 4.22; 95% CI 1.19–14.97; nominal P=0.0259), but not Phase II+ precedence. Exclusive-3D genes showed no significant enrichment. Across 26 drugs, Broad 3D dependency correlated with PDO sensitivity (rho=0.420), but its advantage over 2D was uncertain (difference 0.125; drug-bootstrap 95% interval −0.086 to 0.362).

**Conclusions:** Reproducibility, historical development and pharmacologic association represent distinct validity dimensions; these retrospective comparisons do not establish a generic 3D-specific advantage.

## Likely desk-rejection reason

Editorial assessment: the new resource comparison may be judged too descriptive, with insufficient mechanistic insight or direct clinical applicability for BJC. The corrected Phase III+ association is not a substitute for patient outcomes; its secondary status and sparse event base must stay visible. BJC states that bioinformatics should, wherever possible, be supported by experimental results. Existing external PDO pharmacology is relevant but is not new experimental validation by this author.
