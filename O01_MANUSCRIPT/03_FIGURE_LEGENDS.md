## Figure 1. Resource selection and distinct validation layers

The study compares aggregate dependency profiles from 18 Broad CRC 3D screens, 67 lineage-matched Broad adherent 2D screens and 81 Sanger CRC models after exclusion of annotated Broad overlap. The Broad published Figure 5 comparison universe contained 16,691 gene symbols; complete values shared by all three profiles defined the 15,008-gene analysis universe. Historical clinical-precedence analyses used Open Targets 26.06 and 88 CRC disease identifiers. Independent pharmacology originated from a 211-PDO-row, 62-column DSS workbook; the preferred analysis comprised 26 drugs. Statistical units are genes for cross-profile and clinical comparisons and drugs for pharmacologic association. Cohort sizes denote source screens or models, not patient counts. This descriptive flow diagram contains no hypothesis test or CI and does not imply paired identical models or patient-level inference.

## Figure 2. Strongest-decile overlap and its 2D component

Each 3D strongest-decile list contained 1,501 genes from the 15,008-gene shared universe. The 763-gene intersection comprised 645 genes also in the Broad 2D strongest decile and 118 outside it; 349 additional genes were in Broad 2D but outside both 3D strongest-decile lists. The all-three-profile fraction of the 3D intersection was 84.53%. The two-3D co-membership OR was 17.8883 from a one-sided Fisher exact test. The stored overlap P value underflowed numerically; an exact P value is therefore not reported. No OR CI was estimated in the locked overlap analysis. Unit: gene. Categories describe aggregate rank membership, not universal essentiality, culture-format interactions or therapeutic target specificity. The displayed categories are limited to the membership counts in the locked output.

## Figure 3. Cross-resource concordance against a lineage-matched 2D comparator

Spearman correlations across 15,008 genes were 0.3299897370 for Broad CRC 3D versus Sanger CRC 3D and 0.3531986912 for Broad CRC 2D versus Sanger CRC 3D. The directly evaluated difference was −0.0232089542; the conditional gene-bootstrap 95% interval was −0.0330344 to −0.0131187, using 2,000 aligned gene resamples. Pearson companion correlations were 0.5109250 and 0.5181936. Bootstrap intervals were estimated for the correlation difference, not for individual correlations. Cohort sizes were 18 and 67 Broad screens and 81 Sanger models. Unit: gene conditional on fixed cohort-mean profiles. The figure does not estimate a randomized or within-model culture-format effect or establish biological superiority of 2D.

## Figure 4. Historical clinical precedence with corrected event orientation

Unadjusted Phase II-or-higher frequencies were 36/645 for the all-three-profile class and 9/349 for TWO_D_ONLY; Phase III-or-higher frequencies were 27/645 and 3/349. Their two-sided Fisher ORs were 2.2332 and 5.0388, with P=0.03661 and P=0.002901. In the separate exclusive-3D comparison, frequencies were 4/118 versus 9/349 for Phase II-or-higher (OR, 1.3255; P=0.7461), and 0/118 versus 3/349 for Phase III-or-higher (P=0.5751). The adjusted model used 994 gene rows, explicit numeric outcomes, all-three-profile membership, standardized Broad 2D effect and the Open Targets-derived essentiality indicator. Corrected adjusted ORs were 1.3487761406 (Wald 95% CI, 0.5853139188–3.1080707618; P=0.4823844923) and 4.2187090214 (1.1886118578–14.9733537404; nominal P=0.0259240472), with 45 and 30 events. The original highest-stage/approval-coded endpoint had three events and an unstable separated fit; it is described by event counts rather than an interpretable OR. Unit: gene. Secondary retrospective endpoints; no multiplicity-adjusted or causal interpretation. The adjusted contrast is not exclusive-3D versus 2D-only. Correction trace: run 35414237376.

## Figure 5. Exploratory independent PDO pharmacologic association

For 26 mapped inhibitory drugs, strongest-target dependency correlations with median PDO DSS were 0.4200791 (P=0.03263), 0.2947730 (P=0.14378) and 0.3176291 (P=0.11383) for Broad CRC 3D, Broad CRC 2D and Sanger CRC 3D, respectively. The Broad 3D-minus-2D difference was +0.1253061 (10,000-draw paired drug-bootstrap 95% interval, −0.08612 to +0.36223). Median-target correlations were 0.2428916, 0.1959577 and −0.0236423, respectively, with a Broad difference of +0.0469339 (−0.17130 to +0.26653). Existing at-most-three-retained-target sensitivities used 21 drugs and are detailed in Supplementary Table S6. Unit: drug; DSS is a cohort median over available PDO rows. The starting workbook had 211 PDO rows, not 211 independent patients. Intervals resample drugs, not patients or target families. The selected strongest target can differ by resource. Neither separate correlation P values nor positive leave-one-drug-out point estimates establish a between-resource advantage. All pharmacologic comparisons are exploratory.
